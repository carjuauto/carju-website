CARJU JAPAN Services

We help you buy from Japan with confidence.

From finding the right vehicle to bidding, checking, paperwork, and shipping coordination.

What we do for you

CARJU JAPAN works as your buying agent in Japan. We search beyond ordinary stock lists so you can access auction cars, local dealers, private sellers, and special units that fit your budget.

🚗

Auction Bidding

We help you bid at major Japanese vehicle auctions.

We shortlist vehicles, review auction sheets, guide you on realistic bid prices, and only bid after your approval. This helps you avoid rushing into bad units.

🔎

Deep Market Search

We search auctions, dealers, yards, and private sellers.

Many exporters only show their own stock. We search wider so you get more options, better prices, and a better chance of finding the right car.

📸

Condition Checks

We review details before you commit.

We check auction sheets, visible damage, seller information, photos, and available records. If something looks risky, we tell you before money is committed.

📄

Paperwork & Export

We support export documents and shipping paperwork.

We help with invoice preparation, export certificate, deregistration, inspection where required, and Bill of Lading coordination.

💬

Buying Advisory

We guide you on models, prices, and resale sense.

We help you think through what makes sense for Uganda, East Africa, and your target market: fuel, maintenance, parts, resale, and total landed cost.

🚢

Shipping Coordination

We coordinate movement from purchase to port.

We help coordinate inland transport, yard movement, booking support, loading updates, and shipment documents so you know what is happening at each stage.
