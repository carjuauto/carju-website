About CARJU JAPAN

Built in Japan. Trusted by buyers beyond Japan.

We help clients source vehicles from auctions, dealers, and private sellers with clear communication, verified information, and export support.

Our Story

CARJU JAPAN was created to make buying from Japan clearer, safer, and more personal.

Many buyers struggle with limited stock lists, unclear fees, and slow updates. CARJU JAPAN was formed to solve that problem. We work as your agent in Japan, searching auctions, local yards, dealers, and verified private sellers to find the right unit for your needs and budget.

From the first shortlist to shipping, we share photos, auction sheets, condition notes, and costs in plain language.
